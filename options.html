How long the overlay stays on screen, and how long you have between presses to keep rotating. It applies even if the overlay is turned off.

  1. Click Change shortcuts to open the browser's shortcut settings.
  2. Find Previous Tab Switcher and click the box next to the action you want to change.
  3. Press the key combination you want. Include Ctrl or Alt.
Use the first shortcut to toggle between two tabs (A→B, then B→A). Tap the second one repeatedly to rotate through more tabs, then pause to land on one.
